# 风格参考库

## 一、电影导演风格锚定

> ⚠️ 输出脱敏：导演名仅作内部索引，生成提示词时改用下方「特征」列的中性描述，**不写导演真名**（在世导演名可能被下游平台拦截）。

### 韦斯·安德森 (Wes Anderson)
- **特征**：对称构图、柔和粉彩色调、正面对称镜头、扁平透视
- **提示词**：Wes Anderson style, symmetrical composition, pastel color palette, flat perspective, centered framing, whimsical, meticulous set design
- **适用**：温馨喜剧、文艺片、MV

### 克里斯托弗·诺兰 (Christopher Nolan)
- **特征**：写实主义、宏大场面、IMAX级构图、冷色调、复杂叙事
- **提示词**：Christopher Nolan style, epic realistic cinematography, IMAX scale, cool blue tones, practical effects, immersive wide shots
- **适用**：科幻、悬疑、史诗

### 扎克·施奈德 (Zack Snyder)
- **特征**：高对比度慢镜头、油画质感、肌肉美学、暗色调
- **提示词**：Zack Snyder style, high contrast slow motion, painterly aesthetic, desaturated palette, dramatic speed ramps, heroic framing
- **适用**：超级英雄、奇幻动作、MV

### 王家卫 (Wong Kar-wai)
- **特征**：浓郁饱和色、失焦霓虹、手持风格、慢镜头+抽帧
- **提示词**：Wong Kar-wai style, saturated neon colors, motion blur, step-printed slow motion, handheld intimacy, nostalgic rain-slicked streets
- **适用**：都市爱情、文艺、情绪片段

### 宫崎骏 / 吉卜力 (Studio Ghibli)
- **特征**：细腻手绘感、饱和自然色、柔软光影、自然细节
- **提示词**：Studio Ghibli style, hand-drawn animation, soft natural lighting, lush green nature, detailed backgrounds, nostalgic warmth
- **适用**：奇幻、治愈、自然

### 新海诚 (Makoto Shinkai)
- **特征**：超写实光影、绚丽天空、细腻光晕、青春感
- **提示词**：Makoto Shinkai style, photorealistic lighting, dramatic skies, lens flare, vibrant color grading, youthful atmosphere
- **适用**：青春爱情、唯美场景

### 蒂姆·波顿 (Tim Burton)
- **特征**：哥特暗黑、夸张造型、苍白妆容、扭曲空间
- **提示词**：Tim Burton style, gothic whimsy, exaggerated proportions, pale characters, twisted architecture, dark fairytale aesthetic
- **适用**：黑暗童话、奇幻恐怖

### 黑泽明 (Akira Kurosawa)
- **特征**：动态天气（雨、风）、群像调度、多机位动作、强烈几何构图
- **提示词**：Akira Kurosawa style, dynamic weather, rain and wind, epic group compositions, strong geometric framing, black and white or vivid color
- **适用**：武士、历史、动作

### 库布里克 (Stanley Kubrick)
- **特征**：单点透视、对称构图、冷酷抽离感、精准调度
- **提示词**：Stanley Kubrick style, one-point perspective, clinical symmetry, detached precision, wide angle corridors, eerie stillness
- **适用**：科幻、心理惊悚

## 二、美术风格参考

### 赛博朋克 (Cyberpunk)
```
cyberpunk aesthetic, neon-lit rain-slicked streets, 
holographic advertisements, towering megastructures, 
cybernetic enhancements, high tech low life,
blade runner atmosphere, pink and cyan lighting
```

### 蒸汽朋克 (Steampunk)
```
steampunk aesthetic, brass and copper machinery, 
Victorian fashion, clockwork mechanisms, steam-powered,
industrial revolution, gears cogs pipes,
sepia and warm brass tones
```

### 废土 (Post-Apocalyptic / Wasteland)
```
post-apocalyptic wasteland, Mad Max style,
rusted metal, desert landscape, worn leather,
scavenged technology, dust storm atmosphere,
desaturated earthy tones, harsh sunlight
```

### 奇幻中世纪 (Fantasy Medieval)
```
fantasy medieval, epic castle architecture,
torch-lit halls, stone and wood textures,
knights and sorcery, mystical atmosphere,
warm firelight, high fantasy aesthetic
```

### 太空歌剧 (Space Opera)
```
space opera, vast cosmic scale, nebula clouds,
futuristic starship interiors, holographic interfaces,
deep space atmosphere, starfield background,
clean sci-fi aesthetic, contrast between light and shadow
```

### 黑色电影 (Film Noir)
```
film noir style, black and white, high contrast,
venetian blind shadows, rain-soaked streets,
1940s detective aesthetic, cigarette smoke,
dramatic chiaroscuro lighting
```

### 洛可可 / 巴洛克 (Rococo / Baroque)
```
rococo style, ornate gold decorations, pastel colors,
elaborate costumes, opulent palace interiors,
soft candlelight, extravagant detail,
aristocratic elegance
```

### 极简主义 (Minimalist)
```
minimalist aesthetic, clean lines, negative space,
geometric simplicity, limited color palette,
modern architecture, zen-like calm,
Scandinavian design influence
```

### 水墨中国风 (Chinese Ink Wash)
```
Chinese ink wash painting style, sumi-e,
monochrome or subtle color, expressive brushwork,
cloud and mist atmosphere, mountain landscape,
zen aesthetic, rice paper texture
```

### 敦煌壁画风 (Dunhuang Mural)
```
Dunhuang mural style, flying apsaras, 
mineral pigment colors, aged fresco texture,
Buddhist art influence, flowing ribbons,
gold and turquoise palette, ancient elegance
```

## 三、艺术家风格参考

### 概念艺术
- **Craig Mullins**：电影级概念图，戏剧性光影
- **Sparth**：科幻场景，大胆几何形状
- **John Berkey**：太空飞船，松散笔触，巨大尺度感
- **Syd Mead**：未来城市，工业化设计，细节密集

### 插画/漫画
- **Moebius**：清晰线条，超现实景观，科幻
- **Yoshitaka Amano**：飘逸线条，吸血鬼美学，灵动笔触
- **James Jean**：精细插画，超现实，柔和色彩
- **Hayao Miyazaki**：自然主义，柔软角色，绿色调

### 摄影
- **Ansel Adams**：黑白风景，极致影调
- **Gregory Crewdson**：电影感布光，孤独郊区场景
- **Henri Cartier-Bresson**：决定性瞬间，人文街拍

### 古典绘画
- **Rembrandt**：伦勃朗光，深褐色调
- **Caravaggio**：极端明暗对比，戏剧性
- **Monet**：印象派，光影碎片
- **Hokusai**：浮世绘，平面构图
