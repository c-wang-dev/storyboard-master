# 光影与色彩

## 一、光线方向

| 光位 | 英文 | 效果 | 提示词关键词 |
|------|------|------|------------|
| 顺光 | Front Light | 正面均匀照亮，立体感弱 | front lit, flat lighting, evenly illuminated |
| 侧光 | Side Light | 强烈明暗对比，突出肌理 | side lit, dramatic shadows, texture emphasis |
| 逆光 | Back Light / Rim Light | 轮廓勾勒，剪影效果 | backlit, rim lighting, silhouette, hair light glow |
| 顶光 | Top Light | 上方照射，戏剧性阴影 | top lit, overhead lighting, dramatic brow shadows |
| 底光 | Bottom Light | 下方照射，诡异恐怖感 | bottom lit, underlighting, eerie glow from below |
| 45度侧光 | Rembrandt Lighting | 经典人像光，鼻侧三角形阴影 | Rembrandt lighting, triangle shadow on cheek |
| 蝴蝶光 | Butterfly / Paramount Lighting | 鼻下蝴蝶形阴影，美人光 | butterfly lighting, glamour portrait, nose shadow |

## 二、光质分类

### 硬光 (Hard Light)
- 锐利阴影、高对比度、肌理强烈
- **提示词**：hard light, crisp shadows, high contrast, intense highlights
- **场景**：正午日光、聚光灯、警匪片、黑色电影

### 柔光 (Soft Light)
- 柔和过渡、低对比度、皮肤美化
- **提示词**：soft diffused light, gentle shadows, flattering wrap-around light
- **场景**：阴天、柔光箱、浪漫爱情、MV风格

### 环境光 (Ambient Light)
- 自然散射光、均匀照明
- **提示词**：ambient lighting, natural illumination, environmental bounce light

### 实用光源 (Practical Light)
- 画面中可见的光源（灯、烛、霓虹）
- **提示词**：practical lighting, visible light source, diegetic illumination

## 三、特殊光影类型

### 黄金时刻 (Golden Hour)
- 日出后/日落前1小时，温暖金色光
- **提示词**：golden hour lighting, warm amber glow, long dramatic shadows, sunflare

### 蓝色时刻 (Blue Hour)
- 日出前/日落后，冷蓝调
- **提示词**：blue hour atmosphere, twilight blue tones, soft cool ambient light

### 体积光 / 丁达尔效应 (Volumetric Light / God Rays)
- 光束穿过尘埃/雾可见
- **提示词**：volumetric lighting, god rays, light beams through atmosphere, crepuscular rays

### 霓虹灯光 (Neon Lighting)
- 赛博朋克标志性光源
- **提示词**：neon lighting, colored gel lights, cyberpunk illumination, pink and cyan glow

### 烛光 / 火光 (Candlelight / Firelight)
- 温暖摇曳的暖光
- **提示词**：candlelit scene, warm firelight glow, flickering illumination, intimate lighting

### 月光 (Moonlight)
- 冷蓝色夜间照明
- **提示词**：moonlit scene, cool blue night light, silver illumination, nocturnal atmosphere

### 雾/烟中的光 (Light through Fog)
- 光线穿过雾散射
- **提示词**：light piercing through fog, hazy atmosphere, diffused beam

### 剪影 (Silhouette)
- 主体全黑，只留轮廓
- **提示词**：silhouette against bright background, backlit figure, stark contrast

### 伦勃朗光 (Rembrandt)
- 经典三角光
- **提示词**：Rembrandt lighting portrait, chiaroscuro, triangle of light on shadowed cheek

### 高调光 (High Key)
- 明亮、低对比度、欢快
- **提示词**：high key lighting, bright and airy, minimal shadows, cheerful atmosphere

### 低调光 (Low Key)
- 暗调、高对比度、神秘/紧张
- **提示词**：low key lighting, deep shadows, moody atmosphere, noir style

## 四、色彩情绪

### 暖色调 (Warm Tones)
| 颜色 | 情绪 | 提示词 |
|------|------|--------|
| 红 | 激情、危险、力量 | warm red tones, passionate atmosphere, intense energy |
| 橙 | 温暖、活力、怀旧 | orange warmth, nostalgic glow, energetic feel |
| 黄 | 希望、欢乐、警示 | golden yellow light, cheerful warmth, optimistic tone |
| 琥珀 | 复古、温暖、亲密 | amber tones, vintage warmth, cozy atmosphere |

### 冷色调 (Cool Tones)
| 颜色 | 情绪 | 提示词 |
|------|------|--------|
| 蓝 | 冷静、忧郁、科技 | cool blue palette, melancholic mood, futuristic atmosphere |
| 青 | 清新、未来、疏离 | cyan-teal tones, clinical atmosphere, sci-fi aesthetic |
| 紫 | 神秘、高贵、梦幻 | purple tones, mystical atmosphere, dreamlike quality |
| 绿 | 自然、病态、诡异 | green tint, sickly atmosphere, nature palette |

## 五、氛围描述综合示例

### 悬疑/紧张
```
low key lighting, deep shadows, single practical light source, 
chiaroscuro effect, cool blue undertones, cinematic tension
```

### 浪漫/唯美
```
golden hour backlight, soft diffused light, warm amber tones, 
shallow depth of field, dreamy bokeh, romantic atmosphere
```

### 末日/废土
```
harsh sunlight, desaturated palette, dusty atmosphere, 
bleak landscape, high contrast, post-apocalyptic lighting
```

### 科幻/未来
```
cool blue and cyan tones, neon accent lights, clean sterile lighting, 
holographic reflections, futuristic ambient glow
```

### 温馨/治愈
```
warm interior lighting, soft natural window light, cozy atmosphere, 
comfortable shadows, gentle golden tones, intimate setting
```
